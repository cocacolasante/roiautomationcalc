# Blueprint ROI Calculator — User Guide

## What Is Blueprint ROI Calculator?

Blueprint ROI Calculator is a short online assessment your potential clients take to discover how much time and money they are losing to manual work in their business. The tool asks a series of questions, calculates a real dollar figure for the savings automation could deliver, and generates a personalized audit report with specific recommendations.

## Who Is It For?

- Sales teams and consultants who need a compelling, data-driven way to open conversations about automation with prospects
- Businesses that want to demonstrate value to potential clients before a sales call, by showing them their own numbers
- Agencies that resell automation services and need a branded tool to qualify and educate leads

## Key Concepts

- **Tenant** — your business's workspace within Blueprint ROI Calculator. Your tool configuration, completed audits, and leads are all stored here.
- **Audit** — the completed assessment a prospect submits, including their answers and the resulting savings calculation.
- **ROI Estimate** — the calculated annual dollar amount a prospect could save by automating the manual workflows identified in the assessment.
- **Audit Report** — the AI-generated document summarizing the prospect's pain points, estimated savings, and recommended solutions. It is generated after the assessment is submitted.
- **Lead** — a prospect who has completed the audit. Their contact information and audit results are stored in your dashboard.
- **Embed** — the way Blueprint ROI Calculator appears on your website — as a self-contained tool embedded directly on your page.

## Getting Started

1. Log in to your Blueprint portal using the credentials you received by email.
2. Navigate to Blueprint ROI Calculator in your portal.
3. Go to Configuration and review the assessment questions. Your Blueprint partner will have configured these to match your service focus.
4. Confirm your branding settings — your business name and colors are applied to the tool automatically.
5. Set up your CRM integration in Settings if you want audit leads to sync to HubSpot or GoHighLevel.
6. Copy the embed code from the Embed section of your dashboard.
7. Install the embed code on your website — a landing page, services page, or dedicated calculator page all work well.
8. Test the tool by completing a sample audit yourself and confirming the report generates correctly.

## Core Features

### Interactive Assessment

Prospects complete a structured questionnaire that identifies the manual processes in their business and estimates time spent on each.

- 5-10 questions covering common manual workflows: scheduling, invoicing, follow-up, data entry, and more
- Questions are plain English and take 3-5 minutes to complete
- A live ROI counter updates on screen as the prospect answers each question — they see their savings number growing in real time
- The assessment is fully mobile-friendly and embedded directly on your website

### ROI Calculation

After the assessment, Blueprint ROI Calculator produces a personalized savings estimate based on the prospect's answers.

- The calculation accounts for hours spent, team size, and typical labor costs for their role and industry
- Results show annual savings in dollars — a concrete, credible number
- The calculation methodology is visible to the prospect so the number feels earned, not invented
- Results are displayed on a results page immediately after submission

[Screenshot: Results page showing large annual savings figure, breakdown of time saved per task, and call-to-action button]

### AI-Generated Audit Report

Each completed assessment generates a personalized written report summarizing the findings and recommending specific solutions.

- Reports are written in plain English — no jargon, no generic content
- Each report addresses the specific workflows the prospect identified as manual
- Recommendations are aligned with the solutions your business offers
- The report can be downloaded as a PDF or viewed online

### Lead Capture and Management

Every prospect who completes the assessment becomes a lead in your dashboard.

- Name, email, and company are collected during the assessment
- Leads appear in your dashboard immediately after submission
- Each lead is linked to their full audit results and generated report
- You receive a notification when a new lead completes an assessment

Track how the tool is being used and how many leads it is generating.

- Total assessment starts, completions, and drop-off rate

## Step-by-Step Walkthroughs

### Installing the Calculator on Your Website

1. Log in to Blueprint ROI Calculator and go to the Embed section.
2. Copy the embed code snippet.
3. Open your website editor and navigate to the page where you want the calculator to appear.
4. In the HTML or embed section of that page, paste the code.
5. Save and publish the page.
6. Visit the page in a browser and confirm the calculator loads correctly.
7. Complete a test assessment yourself to confirm the full flow works — assessment, results, and report.

Outcome: The ROI Calculator is live on your website and ready to assess visitor businesses.

### Completing a Test Assessment

1. Open the page where the calculator is embedded.
2. Enter test contact information — use your own name and email.
3. Answer each question honestly as if you were describing a real business.
4. Watch the live ROI counter update as you answer.
5. Submit the assessment.
6. View the results page — note the annual savings figure and the breakdown.
7. Download the audit report PDF and review its content.
8. Go back to your Blueprint ROI Calculator dashboard and confirm the lead appeared.

Outcome: You understand exactly what the prospect experiences and can describe it confidently in your sales conversations.

### Following Up with a New Lead

1. You receive a notification that a prospect has completed an assessment.
2. Log in to your Blueprint ROI Calculator dashboard and go to Leads.
3. Click on the new lead to view their profile.
4. Review their audit results — see which manual workflows they identified and their total savings estimate.
5. Download or open their audit report to review the AI's recommendations.
6. Reach out to the prospect by email or phone. Reference their specific savings figure in your opening — "I saw you completed our ROI assessment and the results showed $47,000 in potential annual savings — I wanted to reach out and discuss how we can help you capture that."
7. Use the audit report as a leave-behind or talking point during your sales conversation.

Outcome: You open the sales conversation with the prospect's own data, making it immediately relevant and credible.

### Reviewing Assessment Analytics

1. Go to Analytics in your Blueprint ROI Calculator dashboard.
2. Review the completion rate — what percentage of people who start the assessment finish it.
3. If the completion rate is low, check whether the questions are too lengthy or unclear.
4. Review the average ROI estimate — this tells you how significant the automation opportunity is for your average prospect.
5. Review lead volume over time to see whether the calculator is generating consistent leads.
6. Contact your Blueprint partner to adjust the questions if you want to improve completion rates or relevance.

Outcome: You have data to optimize the assessment and maximize lead generation from the tool.

## Settings & Configuration

**Assessment Questions** — The questions shown to prospects during the assessment. These are configured by your Blueprint partner to align with the services you offer. Changes to questions require contact with your Blueprint partner.

**Branding** — Your business name and color scheme applied to the calculator and report. Keep this consistent with your website for a seamless experience.

**CRM Integration** — Your connected CRM for syncing completed leads. Set up field mappings carefully so audit data lands in the right fields in your CRM.

**Notification Settings** — Where you receive alerts when new assessments are completed. Email notifications are the default; SMS is available.

**Report Settings** — Controls over what appears in the generated audit report. Contact your Blueprint partner to adjust the structure or content focus of reports.

**Embed Configuration** — Settings for how the calculator appears on your website — full width, fixed height, or other display options.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Place the calculator on a dedicated landing page you can send to prospects rather than burying it on a secondary page. A standalone URL is easier to share and promotes more intentional completions.
- Mention the calculator in your sales outreach: "Before our call, take 5 minutes to complete our ROI assessment — the results will make our conversation more specific and useful for you."
- Include the calculator link in follow-up emails after networking events or initial prospect conversations.
- Review new leads the same day they complete the assessment. The prospect's interest level is highest immediately after seeing their results.
- Use the prospect's specific savings number when you reach out. "We could help you save $52,000 a year" is far more compelling than general automation benefits.
- Do not wait for prospects to contact you after completing the assessment. Most will not — be the one to reach out first.
- Share sample audit reports with prospects who have not yet completed the assessment. Seeing the output often motivates them to take the assessment.
- Update your Blueprint partner if your service offerings change significantly. The recommendations in the report should reflect what you actually offer.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** The calculator is not loading on my website.
**Solution:** Confirm the embed code was pasted into an HTML section of your page, not a plain text block. Check that your website was published after adding the code. Clear your browser cache and reload the page.

**Problem:** The assessment completes but no report is generated.
**Solution:** Contact your Blueprint partner. Report generation requires the AI service to be active and configured. This is not something you can fix from the dashboard.

**Problem:** New leads are not appearing after assessments are completed.
**Solution:** Check your dashboard for new leads by removing any active filters. If the assessment is completing but leads are not appearing, contact your Blueprint partner.

**Problem:** The ROI number seems too high or too low for the assessments being completed.
**Solution:** The ROI calculation is based on the configuration your Blueprint partner set up. If the estimates are not realistic for your target audience, contact your Blueprint partner to review the calculation parameters.

**Problem:** Leads are not syncing to my CRM.
**Solution:** Check the CRM integration status in Settings. Reconnect if the status shows disconnected. Verify your field mappings are complete.

**Problem:** The audit report is recommending services I do not offer.
**Solution:** The recommendations in the report are generated based on your configured service offerings. Contact your Blueprint partner to update the report configuration to match your current service catalog.

## FAQs

**How long does the assessment take for a prospect to complete?**
The assessment takes 3-5 minutes on average. It is designed to be short enough to complete in a single sitting while still gathering enough information for a meaningful ROI calculation.

**Can I customize the assessment questions?**
The questions are configured by your Blueprint partner based on your service focus. To add, remove, or change questions, contact your Blueprint partner.

**Is the ROI estimate a guaranteed amount?**
No. The ROI estimate is based on the information the prospect provides and general industry benchmarks. It is an estimate designed to illustrate the potential savings from automation, not a financial guarantee.

**Can I see the full assessment a prospect submitted?**
Yes. Click on any lead in your dashboard to see their full assessment responses alongside the generated audit report.

**Can I embed the calculator in multiple places on my website?**
Yes. The same embed code can be placed on multiple pages. All completed assessments flow into the same lead list in your dashboard.

**Does the prospect receive a copy of their audit report?**
Yes. The prospect can download the PDF report from the results page immediately after completing the assessment. You also have access to the report from their lead record in your dashboard.

**What happens if a prospect starts the assessment but does not finish?**
Incomplete assessments are not captured as leads. The prospect only appears in your dashboard after they complete and submit the full assessment.

**Can I add a follow-up email that sends automatically after a prospect completes the assessment?**
Automatic follow-up email after assessment completion is not a built-in feature of Blueprint ROI Calculator. However, if you have Blueprint Pipeline connected, leads can be enrolled in a follow-up sequence automatically. Contact your Blueprint partner to set this up.

**How many leads does the calculator typically generate per month?**
Lead volume depends entirely on how much traffic your website receives and where you promote the calculator. A good benchmark is 5-10% of visitors who reach the calculator page will complete the assessment.

**Can I white-label the calculator with my own branding instead of Blueprint branding?**
Yes. The calculator displays your business name and branding. Blueprint branding is not visible to your prospects.
